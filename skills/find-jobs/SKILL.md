---
name: find-jobs
description: Find current job listings for a JobLoop candidate, record discovered listings and score them through MCP.
---
Use the single get_task_context result from task start for the candidate profile and assigned source; do not call it again within the task. Use list_applications only when full history is needed; add_job also checks duplicates. Search with available search/browser tools across official employer careers pages, LinkedIn, ATS boards and relevant local specialist sites. Vary queries by role synonyms and adjacent experience supported by the CV.
When get_task_context names a configured source, search only that source and its saved scope for this bounded task. Do not silently replace it with an easier platform. The source URL is the discovery boundary; record the canonical employer or ATS listing URL when available.
Identify the actual individual listing and save it with add_job even when its fit is weak or uncertain; do not discard discovered listings because of fit. Open the employer listing to evaluate it. Search snippets are discovery evidence, not proof that applications are open. Check the apply link, location, remote country restrictions and mandatory credentials/languages. Treat unstated pay as unknown, not a rejection or a reason to question the candidate.
Read .agents/skills/rank-jobs/SKILL.md and use record_job_rank after saving each listing. Preserve low scores and uncertainties in the app. Use add_job with company, role, canonical listing URL, location and a concise fit explanation including significant gaps. Prefer the original employer vacancy over mirrored copies. Check listing IDs as well as company, role and location; a tracking URL or second platform is not a new vacancy. A duplicate response means reuse the existing record, never restart a submitted application.
In a campaign search task, record listings without applying; the scheduler assigns applications separately. Keep coverage broad, but do not lower hard requirements or fabricate skills to hit a numerical target.
Continue to the candidate's stopping condition or a concrete blocker. If tools are unavailable, report the missing capability through ask_candidate rather than claiming to have searched.

For a configured source call get_source_instructions with its ID. Follow its skillText, workflow, searchMethod, toolReference and fallback. In tool mode use run_source_tool with documented arguments from the profile. Free mode permits independent web search; browser mode uses browser tools. A failed tool is not zero results. After opening/navigating a search tab save_source_checkpoint with its actual browser, stable tab ID and URL. Keep the assigned source; report limitations honestly.

Close search and listing tabs you opened once their research is finished. With existing browser tools, close only your own completed research tabs before reporting the task. Keep unfinished application forms, pending access/login steps and user-owned tabs open. In Jev mode, completed source-search task tabs are closed automatically after report_campaign_work returns success; do not issue extra cleanup calls after reporting.
