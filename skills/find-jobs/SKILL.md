---
name: find-jobs
description: Find current job listings for a JobLoop candidate, verify suitability and record unique opportunities through MCP.
---
Read the candidate profile and application history through MCP. Search with available search/browser tools across official employer careers pages, LinkedIn, ATS boards and relevant local specialist sites. Vary queries by role synonyms and adjacent experience supported by the CV.
When get_campaign names a configured source, search only that source and its saved scope for this bounded task. Do not silently replace it with an easier platform. The source URL is the discovery boundary; record the canonical live employer or ATS listing URL when available.
Open the live employer listing before recording it. Search snippets are discovery evidence, not proof that applications are open. Check the apply link, location, remote country restrictions and mandatory credentials/languages. Treat unstated pay as unknown; ask for a reusable preference if salary is a hard minimum.
Use add_job with company, role, canonical listing URL, location and a concise fit explanation including significant gaps. Prefer the original employer vacancy over mirrored copies. Check listing IDs as well as company, role and location; a tracking URL or second platform is not a new vacancy. A duplicate response means reuse the existing record, never restart a submitted application.
Use apply-to-jobs when an open role fits the candidate's constraints. Continue independent searches while a form is blocked. Keep coverage broad, but do not lower hard requirements or fabricate skills to hit a numerical target.
Continue to the candidate's stopping condition or a concrete blocker. If tools are unavailable, report the missing capability through ask_candidate rather than claiming to have searched.
